# Product-led growth and product-qualified leads

Load for self-serve products with usage data, freemium, or trials.

## Core idea

In PLG the product does the first discovery: usage reveals pain, fit and intent. Sales assists at the right moment with the right tone instead of pitching cold.

## Freemium and trials as low-risk entry

- Free tier or trial is the risk-reversal mechanism (`references/guarantees-scarcity.md`); it works when the user reaches a real result without talking to anyone.
- **Activation** is the metric: the first moment the user gets value (the "aha" event). Define it concretely (e.g., "invited a teammate and ran the first report").
- In-product onboarding should drive to activation in the first session; everything else is secondary.

## Product-qualified lead (PQL)

A user or account whose behavior predicts purchase. Define from data, not intuition. Typical signals:

| Signal | Means |
|---|---|
| Invited colleagues | Team adoption, expansion potential |
| Hit a plan limit | Willingness to pay is being tested |
| Used a key feature repeatedly | Found the core value |
| Returned N days in a week | Habit forming |
| Visited pricing page | Active evaluation |
| Admin or decision-maker title signed up | Buying authority present |

Score accounts on a combination; set the PQL threshold where conversion rate clearly rises, and re-check quarterly.

## Sales-assist, not sales-pitch

Timing: within hours of the signal, not days.
Tone: consultative. "I saw your team hit the limit on X. Teams at that point usually want Y; want me to show you how, or set up the plan that removes the limit?"
Never: generic demo invitations to users who are already using the product.

## Expansion is the main revenue line

In healthy PLG, net revenue retention above 100% means expansion (more seats, higher tier, add-ons) outpaces churn. Retention and expansion deserve more effort than acquisition once activation works.

Levers: usage-based pricing tiers that grow with value, team features that pull in colleagues, periodic value recaps ("your team saved X hours this month").

## Metrics (see `references/metrics.md`)

Activation rate → PQL rate → PQL-to-paid conversion → NRR → cohort retention. CAC in PLG includes the cost of serving free users.

## Anti-patterns

- Free tier with no path to value (users leave before activation).
- Sales emailing every signup.
- PQL thresholds set by what sales can handle rather than by conversion data.
- Optimizing acquisition while cohort retention is falling.
