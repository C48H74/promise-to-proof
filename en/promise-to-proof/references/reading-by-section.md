# Reading by section

Read for the decision at hand. This is a study hub and attribution map, not a claim to have read each full book or permission to reproduce it. Use authorized editions. Public pointers and limits are in [sources](sources.md).

| Workflow area | Book / author or practitioner | Study question | Apply with |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 Context | *The Mom Test* — Rob Fitzpatrick | What real behavior supports this hypothesis? | Diagnostic and customer interviews |
| 1 Problem/discovery | *SPIN Selling* — Neil Rackham; *Gap Selling* — Keenan; *The Mom Test* | Which problem/mechanism/gap does the buyer recognize? | Discovery guide and transcript |
| 1 Qualification | *MEDDICC* — Andy Whyte; MEDDPICC practitioner material | Which value, stakeholder and process evidence is missing? | Scorecard and committee map |
| 2 Offer | *$100M Offers* — Alex Hormozi | Which value lever matters here, and what can we control? | Offer canvas |
| 3 Pricing | *Hourly Billing Is Nuts* — Jonathan Stark; Skok's metrics essays | How do value, cost, capacity and cash interact? | Pricing/economics and numerical examples |
| 4 Risk | *Influence* — Robert Cialdini; *$100M Offers* | Which truthful proof or commitment reduces the actual risk? | Risk checklist |
| 5 Limits | *Influence* — Robert Cialdini | Is the scarcity true and decision-relevant? | Verified limits and alternative dates |
| 6 Sales/committee | *The Challenger Sale* — Matthew Dixon and Brent Adamson; *The Challenger Customer* — Brent Adamson, Matthew Dixon, Pat Spenner and Nick Toman | How do we support consensus around a useful insight? | Narrative and multithreading |
| 6 Indecision | *The JOLT Effect* — Matthew Dixon and Ted McKenna | Is this fear of a wrong choice, weak fit or refusal? | JOLT and objection library |
| 6 Negotiation | *Never Split the Difference* — Chris Voss with Tahl Raz | What tradeoffs, constraints and conditional exchanges matter? | Negotiation planner |
| 7 Agreement | ADALID Abogados — v1 practitioner attribution, no specific book asserted | Which terms need authorized local review? | Commercial agreement checklist |
| 8 Referrals | *The Referral Engine* — John Jantsch | Which demonstrated value makes an introduction useful? | Referral request |
| 9 Proof | Joel Klettke — v1 practitioner attribution, no specific book asserted | Which baseline/result/approval supports publication? | Case-study template |
| 10 Website/story | *Building a StoryBrand* — Donald Miller; Andy Raskin; *Content Chemistry* — Andy Crestodina | Which question does this page resolve for the buyer? | Narrative and content plan |
| 10 AI | *AI Snake Oil* — Arvind Narayanan and Sayash Kapoor | What capability is established, and how would we falsify the claim? | AI evaluation and brand audit |
| 11 SaaS/retention | *Product-Led Growth* — Wes Bush; David Skok's metrics essays | Which first useful result predicts durable value? | PLG experiment and cohorts |
| 11 Services/founder | Russell Raath and Harrison Baron — v1 practitioner attribution | Which work creates fit, contribution and reusable proof? | Service example and selection review |

Framework tension is useful: SPIN is not a license for endless discovery, JOLT does not bypass due diligence, Challenger does not mean aggression, and value pricing does not erase delivery cost. Explain the choice of lens and test it against the actual buyer rather than treating authors as universal authority.
