# A referral system after value

Use [request template](../templates/referral-request.md). The v1's Jantsch influence is retained as provenance; this module is an original operating synthesis, not a claim to have verified a specific passage [reading map](reading-by-section.md).

Choose a real useful result as the request trigger. Verify the customer is satisfied; asking at a painful unresolved moment can erode trust. Define the fit narrowly enough that a customer recognizes who might benefit.

Ask whether they know someone with that situation, offer a short optional introduction and make declining easy. Do not imply a duty to reciprocate. Obtain permission before sharing contact details or using names/logos.

Assign one owner to acknowledge the introduction, handle the prospect respectfully and close the loop with appropriate non-confidential information. Stop follow-up on refusal. If an incentive exists, disclose it and check applicable professional/conflict restrictions; never exchange it for a positive testimonial.

Track requested/accepted introductions, qualified opportunities, contribution and referred-customer experience. A referral count without quality can reward bad-fit volume. Keep requested testimonials, introductions and case-study publication permissions separate.

Review the customer experience that generated referrals: first useful result, handoff, issue resolution and visible evidence. Fix delivery before scaling the request cadence. Avoid pretending that a "delight" tactic substitutes for reliable work.
