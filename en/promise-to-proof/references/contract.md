# Commercial agreement preparation

Use [contract handoff](../checklists/contract-handoff.md). This module prepares a commercial brief for authorized parties/local counsel; it does not determine legal enforceability, tax treatment or regulated obligations.

Gather parties and signers, jurisdiction/market, customer type, scope, dependencies, acceptance, exclusions, milestones, price, payment, expenses and taxes needing confirmation. Identify ownership/licensing of outputs and pre-existing materials, confidentiality, data responsibilities, security requirements, support, changes, term/renewal, cancellation and exit/data return.

Check consistency: proposal, guarantee, SLA, invoice/payment schedule and sales statements must describe the same obligation. Refunds, service credits, rework and liability terms can interact; flag contradictions for review. "All sales final" cannot be assumed to override mandatory rights.

For performance fees, specify measurement, baseline, attribution boundaries, audit access, settlement and dispute path. For uncertain work, specify estimates, caps and change approval. For referrals/incentives, check disclosure and conflict requirements in the relevant context.

Record open terms with issue, commercial impact, owner and decision date. Separate a scope negotiation from a legal interpretation. Escalate material ambiguity rather than inventing boilerplate remedies or treating an educational template as a final contract.

Execution readiness: approved scope, feasible staffing/start, authorized signers, required procurement checks, payment terms and dependencies. An agent completing this checklist has not signed, accepted or submitted any agreement.
