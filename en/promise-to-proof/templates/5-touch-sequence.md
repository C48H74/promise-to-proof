# 5-touch sequence (10 days, multichannel)

Fill the brackets from your research. Keep emails under 90 words. One ask per touch. Plain text.

Prerequisites: domain authenticated (SPF/DKIM/DMARC), warmed up, volume capped (`references/outbound.md`).

## Day 1 — Call
Script: `cold-call-scripts.md` (permission opener). Voicemail, if any, 15 seconds: name, one-line reason tied to the signal, "I'll send a short email."

## Day 2 — Email 1
**Subject:** [signal in 3–5 words, e.g. "new ops lead + reporting"]

[First name],

Saw [specific, true, recent signal]. When [type of company] goes through that, [specific problem] usually shows up within [timeframe] — [one-line why].

We helped [similar company or role] [outcome with a number] by [mechanism in five words].

Is [problem] on your radar, or am I off?

[Name]

## Day 4 — LinkedIn
Connection note (under 200 characters): "[First name], saw [signal]. I work with [role] on [problem]. No pitch; happy to compare notes."
Or: a substantive comment on a recent post of theirs.

## Day 7 — Call + Email 2
Call: "I emailed about [problem]; calling because [new fact or insight]. 30 seconds?"

**Subject:** Re: [Email 1 subject]

[First name],

One more thing on [problem]: [one new insight, number or short proof — different from email 1].

If it's useful, I can walk you through how [similar company] handled it in 15 minutes. Worth a look, or wrong timing?

[Name]

## Day 10 — Breakup email
**Subject:** Should I close the file?

[First name],

I've reached out a few times about [problem]; no answer usually means not a priority or wrong person, both fine.

If it's the latter, who should I talk to? If it's the former, I'll check back in [quarter].

[Name]
