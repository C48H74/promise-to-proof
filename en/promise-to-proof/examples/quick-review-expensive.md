# Example: quick review of "it's expensive"

**Request:** "my client says R$ 12,000/year is expensive. What do I answer?"

**Mode:** quick review. **Modules:** `references/objections.md`, `references/qualification.md` (Metrics), `references/jolt.md`.

> **Verdict:** this is not a price objection. It is unquantified value: nobody has put a number on the other side of the scale.
>
> **What to do, in this order:**
> 1. Do not defend the price. An absolute number with no comparison always looks expensive.
> 2. Go back to the pain and quantify it: "how many hours a week does your team spend issuing invoices manually?" Hours x cost per hour x 52 = their number.
> 3. Bring proof from a client of the same size, with authorisation (`references/proof.md`).
> 4. If they still hesitate, it is risk, not price: offer a way out, not a discount (`references/jolt.md`).
> 5. Review the tiers: maybe the complete tier was offered to someone who needs the essential one (`references/offer-pricing.md`).
>
> **Evidence needed:** their pain number and one authorised case. Without both, do not respond.
>
> **Trap:** "I can get you a discount" destroys the anchor and does not fix the cause.
>
> **Next:** advance only when the buyer's own number is on the table.
