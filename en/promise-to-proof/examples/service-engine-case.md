# Complete-engine example: bounded technical review service

**Synthetic company and commercial scenario.** No customer traction, engineering certification or measured savings is implied. Currency is illustrative.

## Context and diagnosis (0–1)

One-month inputs: 40 inquiries, 6 fit, 4 proposals, 2 sales. Each project consumes 20 bottleneck hours; monthly available capacity is 100 hours. Inquiry fit is 15%; concluded proposals have 2 wins/2 losses, but sample size is too small for a stable win-rate inference. Both losses are buyer-reported no-fit. Hypothesis: broad targeting creates review burden; alternative: unclear exclusions attract wrong inquiries.

Research: interview consenting buyers about their last handoff, alternatives and selection. Qualify sample availability, actual review need and approval owner. Do not claim the service solves every design issue.

## Offer, economics and risk (2–5)

Offer: a review of a supplied bounded model sample, with a prioritized issue register and acceptance meeting. Excludes design approval and certification. One core option is enough; no forced premium tier.

Price 2,000; variable cost 1,100; allocated acquisition 150. Contribution after acquisition 750; fixed 3,000; break-even 4 projects and capacity 5. Positive economics need four viable jobs/month, not simply more inquiries. Rework/extra scope can invalidate this model.

Guarantee covers correction of omissions against the agreed review checklist, with bounded scope/time/remedy. Exposure must be approved before offering. Start capacity is checked at booking; no permanent "two slots" claim.

## Decision and delivery (6–7, 11)

Stage exits: fit confirmed → sample/dependencies ready → scope/price reviewed → authorized agreement → first useful issue register accepted. For a larger account, map reviewer, implementation and procurement roles. A written quote is valid; live review is offered where useful.

Use a two-week delivery milestone plan rather than ninety days. Confirm approved sample/access, evidence of completion, buyer review and change route. Open legal/IP/data terms go to the relevant reviewer; the commercial plan is not a signed agreement.

## Learning, proof and distribution (8–10)

Request optional referrals only after accepted useful work. Capture issue resolution and effort with limitations; do not convert issue counts to avoided accidents or cash savings. A case study needs publication approval. Buying page explains scope, sample requirements, exclusions and standard price.

First experiment: improve the target/trigger and exclusions for the next consenting inquiry cohort; owner is the founder, budget cap 300 and four weeks. Continue if at least 6 of the next 20 inquiries are confirmed fit without increased review time or reduced contribution; stop on capacity/quality or contact-permission failures. These thresholds are **proposed scenario choices**, not established benchmarks. If fewer than 20 arrive, report inconclusive.
