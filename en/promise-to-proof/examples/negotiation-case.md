# Negotiation case — conditional scope

**Synthetic scenario.** Full review: 10,000 price, 5,500 variable cost, 4,500 contribution before acquisition/overhead. Seller's required contribution margin is 35% for this scenario, not a universal rule. Delivery/price authority is available up to the stated alternatives.

Buyer requests 20% off. Seller states full scope/price and pauses, then asks whether the issue is comparison, funding or uncertainty. Buyer confirms an 8,000 budget and says the second site can wait.

**Bad response:** "Let's split the difference at 9,000." It ignores the funding constraint and scope, and creates an arbitrary concession.

**Better:** "We can review one site for 8,000 if the second site is excluded and your team supplies the agreed files. The acceptance criteria remain [specified]. The second site can be quoted later."

Revised variable cost 4,500; contribution 3,500; margin **43.75%**. Retaining full scope at 8,000 would yield 2,500 contribution and **31.25%** margin, below this seller's stated floor. The smaller scope is valid only if it still produces the useful outcome and does not hide mandatory work.

Buyer asks for immediate start. Delivery checks capacity and confirms a real later date; seller offers that date without claiming fictional scarcity. The buyer accepts subject to procurement. Written summary lists scope/exclusions, price, dependencies, proposed start and pending approval. No deal is reported closed yet.
