# Trends to watch (review every 6 months)

Last reviewed: 2026-10-13. These shift faster than the core principles. Verify current figures by search before quoting any number to a client.

## 1. Inbox rules keep tightening
Google and Yahoo bulk-sender rules (from Feb 2024) require authentication, easy unsubscribe and low spam complaint rates; enforcement has grown since. Plan for smaller, better-targeted volume. → `references/outbound.md`

## 2. Signal-based selling
Contact people when something changes (hiring, funding, new tool, leadership change, product usage) instead of working static lists. → `references/outbound.md`, `references/plg-pql.md`

## 3. Buying committees are larger and more consensus-driven
Deals stall on "no decision" more than on competitors. Multithreading and a shareable business case are table stakes. → `references/multithreading.md`, `references/jolt.md`

## 4. Buyers research before they talk to you
Reviews, communities, peers and AI assistants shape the shortlist before the first call. Be findable and consistent there; treat the first call as the second or third touch. → `references/content.md`

## 5. AI in the workflow
Useful: call transcription and scoring, research prep, first drafts, CRM hygiene. Risky: unsupervised mass personalization, fabricated claims, buyers detecting generic AI outreach. Keep a human responsible for every claim sent. → `references/ai-in-sales.md`

## 6. Product-led and usage-based motions
Free or low-friction entry, qualify by behavior (PQL), sell expansion to teams. Pricing moves toward usage or outcome components; check margins before copying. → `references/plg-pql.md`, `references/offer-pricing.md`

## 7. Retention as the growth lever
Higher acquisition costs make NRR and time-to-first-value the cheapest growth. → `references/delivery-retention.md`, `references/metrics.md`

## 8. Trust signals under scrutiny
Fake reviews and inflated claims are penalized by platforms and regulators. Verifiable case studies, named references and honest guarantees outperform. → `references/guarantees-scarcity.md`

3. Bump `metadata.last_reviewed` in `SKILL.md` and add a line to `CHANGELOG.md`.
